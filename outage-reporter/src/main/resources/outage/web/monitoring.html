How LogicMonitor watches this site

This outage center exists to show LogicMonitor's hybrid observability on a service that matters to customers. Every layer, from the cloud VM to the business outcome, reports into LogicMonitor, and each kind of failure raises one clear alert.

One alert per cause

Each failure alerts on the measurement that names it. The rest is context, so on-call gets one clear page instead of five.

Business outcomes, not just servers

In a storm every server metric stays green while customers wait. The alert that matters is on the customers waiting for an estimate.

From alerts to answers

Edwin AI groups related alerts into a single insight with a probable root cause, so teams fix the problem instead of triaging noise.

Downed power line? Stay at least 35 feet away and call 1-800-555-0142 right away. Never report a downed line online.
